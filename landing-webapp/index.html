What is Clumo

Ever finished a call and wished you had asked that perfect question or had a better response to that one difficult objection. That is what Clumo is for. It gives you coaching in the call, real time, so you can show up as an A player in every call. Clumo is committed to providing an opensource core.

Who it is for

Built for B2B technology sales people who spend most of their time in complex sales. Non technical and technical sellers both benefit with clearer discovery, stronger control, and better outcomes.

Security stuff for techies

Bring your own key model. Your key is stored locally and encrypted with AES 256 CBC. Clumo does not run a cloud account system and does not sync your key anywhere.

  • Everything runs on your machine. Knowledge base files, transcripts, and settings stay local.
  • The only external connection is directly to your provider, OpenAI or Azure OpenAI, over TLS.
  • During calls, Clumo sends audio for transcription and small transcript excerpts for relevance scoring.
  • After calls, Clumo can send the full transcript once to generate a summary and follow up draft.
  • No telemetry, no tracking, and no data collection by Clumo.

Which meeting tools does it work with

Any meeting tool, as Clumo uses system audio to transcribe and make suggestions. Teams, Zoom, Google, and Slack are all supported.
